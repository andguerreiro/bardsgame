# 🎭 Who Said It? — The Bard’s Game

*A quote appears. Names fall. Catch the one who said it.*

Think you know your Shakespeare? Prove it in seconds.

A famous quote appears at the top of the screen while the names of Shakespeare’s characters rain down from above. Move a tiny pixel-art Shakespeare left and right, **catch the character who said the line**, and **dodge everyone else**.

Was it Hamlet or Polonius? Juliet or Portia? Macbeth or Lady Macbeth? You’ll need quick thinking and quick feet: the names fall vertically at different speeds, and the challenge gets tougher as you play.

## How to play

1. Read the quote at the top of the screen.
2. Catch the name of the character who said it before it hits the floor.
3. Avoid all the other names.
4. Keep catching correct answers to build your streak and boost your score.

You start with **10 lives**. Catch the wrong character, or let the right one hit the floor, and you lose a life.

The game starts gently and gets faster automatically as you make correct catches. There’s no difficulty menu: the challenge progresses through **Easy, Normal and Hard**, with new levels and faster-falling names as you improve.

Each correct catch earns **10 points**, plus a streak bonus of **2 points per consecutive catch**, up to an extra 20 points. Mistakes and missed catches reset your streak, but your overall level progression never goes backwards.

When your lives run out, the curtain falls. Check your final score, share it with your friends and take another bow!

## Controls

- **Computer:** Move with the ← → arrow keys or A / D.
- **Phone or tablet:** Tap the left or right half of the screen to nudge Shakespeare (a longer press moves him farther, and every tap moves him). Hold a side to accelerate smoothly and run in that direction until you let go or reach the edge. Press both sides at once to stop him in place.
- **Touchscreen in landscape:** Taps count anywhere on the screen, including the empty bars around the game. The corner arrows light up while you hold, and both light up when you press both sides.
- **Start or play again:** Press Enter or Space, tap anywhere on the title screen, or choose **Play Again** after a game.
- **Share your score:** Tap **Share Score** on the game-over screen, or press S. Your score and the game link are copied to the clipboard, ready to paste anywhere.
- **Mute or unmute:** Tap the speaker icon, or press M.

The game works on desktop and mobile browsers.

## Features

- **60 famous Shakespeare quotes** from *Hamlet*, *Macbeth*, *Romeo and Juliet*, *Julius Caesar*, *King Lear*, *Othello*, *The Tempest*, *Twelfth Night*, *A Midsummer Night’s Dream*, *The Merchant of Venice*, *As You Like It*, *Richard III*, *Henry IV*, *Henry V*, *Antony and Cleopatra* and more
- **45 characters** to catch or dodge
- A complete theatrical pixel-art presentation, with a hand-built bitmap font, a chunky Shakespeare sprite, curtains, a dithered spotlight, masks, hearts and particle effects
- Names that fall vertically at random speeds
- Automatic difficulty progression: Easy → Normal → Hard
- Streak bonuses and a level system
- Sound effects for catches, mistakes, level-ups and game over, with a mute setting that’s remembered
- Vibration feedback on phones for catches, mistakes, level-ups and game over
- A personal best saved in your browser
- A **Share Score** button that copies your score and the game link to the clipboard
- Free to play in your browser, with no download or sign-up
