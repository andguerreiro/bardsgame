# Who Said It? — The Bard’s Game

*A quote appears. Names fall. Catch the one who said it.*

Think you know your Shakespeare? Prove it in seconds.

A famous quote appears at the top of the screen while the names of Shakespeare’s characters rain down from above. Move a tiny pixel-art Shakespeare left and right, **catch the character who said the line**, and **dodge everyone else**.

Was it Hamlet or Polonius? Juliet or Portia? Macbeth or Lady Macbeth? You’ll need quick thinking and quick feet: the names fall vertically, at different speeds, and the challenge builds as you play.

## How to play

1. Read the quote at the top of the screen.
2. Catch the name of the character who said it.
3. Avoid everyone else.
4. Keep catching correct answers to build your streak and boost your score.

You start with 10 lives. Catch the wrong character, or let the right one hit the floor, and you lose a life.

The game starts gently and gets faster on its own as you make correct catches. There’s no difficulty menu and no pause in the performance, so stay sharp.

When your lives run out, the curtain falls. Check your final score, share it with your friends and take another bow.

## Controls

- **Computer:** move with the ← → arrow keys or A / D. You can also click and drag.
- **Phone or tablet:** drag your finger left and right.
- **Start or play again:** tap the screen, or press Enter or Space.
- **Share your score:** tap **Share Score** on the game-over screen, or press S.
- **Mute or unmute:** tap the speaker icon, or press M.

## Features

- 30 famous Shakespeare quotes from *Hamlet*, *Macbeth*, *Romeo and Juliet*, *Julius Caesar*, *The Tempest*, *Othello* and more
- 39 characters to catch or dodge
- A theatrical pixel-art stage with curtains, a spotlight, masks and particle effects
- Names that fall vertically at different speeds
- Streak bonuses for consecutive correct catches
- Automatic difficulty progression, with faster names as you level up
- Sound effects for catches, mistakes, level-ups and game over, with a mute setting that’s remembered
- A personal best saved in your browser
- A share button to challenge your friends
- Plays in your browser, with no download and no sign-up

## Great for

- Students and teachers looking for a playful way to revisit Shakespeare’s famous lines
- Fans of the Bard who fancy a quick literary challenge
- Anyone who enjoys short arcade games that mix knowledge with reflexes
